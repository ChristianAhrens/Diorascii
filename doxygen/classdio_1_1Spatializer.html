static

Computes the target gain of every channel for a source.

For a stereo layout this is exactly the constant-power law cos/sin(pan * pi/2) the synth has always used, with pan = 0.5 + 0.5 * sin(azimuth). Any other layout uses distance-based amplitude panning (DBAP) on the unit sphere, which needs no triangulation and works for any speaker set: a layout without height speakers simply puts a high source on the speakers nearest to it. spread blends the result with an even distribution. The gains of the directional speakers always satisfy sum(g^2) = 1 (constant power); LFE and silent channels get 0.

Parameters
layoutthe output layout
sourceposition and spread of the sound
gainsreceives layout.channels() values (the rest of kMaxChannels is zeroed)
