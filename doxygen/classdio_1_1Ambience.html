Detailed Description

Generates ambient sound: wind, rain, thunder and crickets at night in the forest, the deep hum, bubbles and passing whales of the aquarium, the dry wind, sandstorms and tornadoes of the desert, the wind, stream and avalanches of the mountains.

Pure computation without I/O, deterministic per seed, so it is testable without a sound device. Parameter changes are smoothed, so switching scenes or muting never clicks.

Where the n bubbles (1 to 3) of one rising cluster are in a room: out[0..n-1], all drawn from rng.

The cluster starts anywhere round the listener, behind included, and every bubble is higher than the one before. Public so that the placement can be tested without listening for bubbles.

The events wait until the next render call; at most kMaxPendingEvents are kept (the newest), and they are dropped after that call. Not used by any sound yet (the scenes switch to it in phase 6 of the multichannel plan); the counters in AmbientParams still trigger them.

void dio::Ambience::render ( const AmbientParams & p,
float * out,
int frames )

Writes frames frames of interleaved float samples, layout().channels() per frame, each within -1 to 1.

The continuous sounds (the bed) play in every full-range speaker with independent noise per speaker; the discrete voices are still a stereo pair (until phases 5 and 6 of the multichannel plan place them) that plays on the layout's front left and right speakers (Layout::stereoPair); the LFE channel plays the low end of the beds. With the stereo layout, and on the front pair of any layout, the output is exactly what the synth has always produced.
