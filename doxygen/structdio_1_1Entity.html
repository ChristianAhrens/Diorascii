◆ StrikeMode

enum class dio::Entity::StrikeMode
strong

How a catastrophe strikes.

Centre: once, all victims at the same moment, when its middle is the strikeAt way across (a storm). Sweep: each victim is taken as the middle of the attacker passes it (an alien beaming up things). Devour: each victim is taken as the front of the attacker reaches it (a sandworm).
