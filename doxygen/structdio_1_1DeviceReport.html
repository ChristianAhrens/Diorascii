◆ exact

bool dio::DeviceReport::exact = false

The positions are the ones the platform itself gives the device's channels (CoreAudio's channel layout of the device), not a default assumption of the audio library.

Only then may a named layout be played by routing its channels to the device's channels of the same position, without the library remapping them (which loses the positions that its own, assumed map lacks).

◆ spatializes

bool dio::DeviceReport::spatializes = false

The route turns a multichannel signal into a spatial rendering itself (AirPods and other spatial-capable headphones, which are two channels to the application).

Automatic then renders the richest layout the spatializer is known to take (7.1.4) instead of a stereo pair, and the system makes the rest.
