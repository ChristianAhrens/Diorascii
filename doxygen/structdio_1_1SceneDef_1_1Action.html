Detailed Description

What the F key does in this scene.

drop <sprite> items fall from the surface (food for the fish) impact <sprite> a meteorite strikes the ground and digs a crater weather <sprite> [<funnel>] rain or a sandstorm for a while (the sprite is the drop or grain); with a funnel sprite, half of the time a tornado crosses the scene instead summon <sprite> a visitor comes through (an ambulance), one at a time
