◆ routed

bool dio::Negotiation::routed = false

Each channel of layout has a device channel of its position, route[i], and the device is not opened again with the layout's map: a backend that places channels by position itself (the Apple backend, which hands the system the layout as tagged PCM) uses the layout alone; route is the placement it makes.

Only on a device whose positions are exact and that has a channel for every channel of the layout (see negotiate()).
