Detailed Description

Everything the interactive program does between input and output: the current scene and world, the ASCII menu, style, time of day, pause, info line, toasts and the sound switch.

It performs no I/O of its own (no terminal, no sound device, no clock), so the terminal frontend and other frontends (such as tvOS) drive it the same way: feed it Actions and elapsed time, draw the Frame it renders, and play the sound it asks for.
