# Privacy

*Last updated: 2026-11-08*

Diorascii does not collect, store, share or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no
analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs, in any version of the program.

## The program (terminal on macOS, Linux and Windows)

- It makes no network connections of its own: no update check, no telemetry, no crash reports.
- It reads nothing from your machine except what you point it at (`--assets DIR`), and writes nothing.
- The ambient sound is generated on the fly and played through your sound device. Diorascii never
  uses a microphone and records nothing.
- Your terminal, operating system or package source may handle data about downloads or launches
  under their own policies (for example, macOS may check the notarization of a downloaded app with Apple
  the first time you open it).

## The tvOS app

- It collects no data and makes no network connections.
- It stores one small setting on the device, in the app's preferences: how many times the app was
  launched, so that the hint about the menu is only shown the first few times. It never leaves the
  device and is deleted with the app.
- Apple distributes the app through the App Store and TestFlight and may provide the developer with
  aggregated, anonymous statistics (downloads, crash reports for users who opted in to share them)
  under Apple's own privacy policy. Diorascii itself receives and keeps no information about you.

## The project page and the repository

- The project page (GitHub Pages) is a static page without third-party scripts, fonts or trackers. It
  remembers your choice of scene and of day or night in your browser (local storage on your device,
  not sent anywhere).
- The page, the downloads and the source code are hosted by GitHub, which may log visits (such as your
  IP address) according to the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
